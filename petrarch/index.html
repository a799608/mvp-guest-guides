🔑Front Door & Pet Gates

Front Door: There is a key for the front door on the kitchen counter with the remote for the LED Bar lights. The lock for the door is a bit tricky. You must turn the lock and the handle at the same time to lock and unlock. Clockwise locks, Counterclockwise unlocks.

Pet Gates: Retractable gate at the front door (to the right, attaches like a window shade) and at the bottom of the interior stairs. Useful for containing pets.

Amenity Badges

🎫Amenity Badges

The community issues one set of badges to be used all year by all guests.

Badges are checked at the pool and beach during normal hours of operation and anyone over the age of 5 must have a badge.

I have seen security ask people who are fishing at the lake to see them, but in general, they are not needed to just be "out and about" the community.

The replacement costs is $250 if any badge is lost. After the 3rd lost badge they suspend amenity use for the remainder of the year. Full forfeiture of your security deposit will occur if any badge is lost.

I am sorry to have to be so strict about this.

House Rules

🚽Septic System

Septic-safe toilet paper only. No wipes, hygiene products, or feminine products. These will cause serious damage to the septic system.

🚫The Nos

  • No smoking inside home (including vaping and marijuana). Outside is fine but please away from open windows.
  • No fireworks ($1000 fine)
  • No noise after 10 PM (quiet hours 10 PM – 8 AM)
Climate & Comfort

🔥Heat

  • Controlled by individual room thermostats
  • Thermostats control heat only
  • Turn off when running A/C

❄️Air Conditioning

  • Multiple window units
  • Run all units on hot days to cool the home

🔥Fireplace

Remote-operated. Press both flame-line buttons (bottom left + top right) simultaneously. Unit beeps and lights in about 20 seconds. Immediately lower the flame or the house will become very hot. Non-operational May through August.

PLEASE DO NOT TOUCH LOG SET FOR ANY REASON. Please do not use the fireplace while running the A/C.
