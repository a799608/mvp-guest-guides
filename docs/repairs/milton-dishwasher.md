## 1. Supply line — buy regardless

Non-copper = braided stainless, which is the current standard. Buy the
**installation kit**, not a bare hose: the kit includes the 90° brass elbow
that threads into the dishwasher's fill valve.

**Spec to ask for:** 6 ft braided stainless dishwasher installation kit,
3/8" compression inlet x 3/8" MIP outlet, elbow included.

| Kit | Spec | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Certified DWKITST (first choice) | 6 ft, 3/8" comp x 3/8" MIP, elbow incl. | ~$22 |
| Eastman 41032 | 6 ft, 3/8" comp x 1/2" FIP, 3/8" MIP elbow | — |
| Eastman 41037 | 4 ft, 3/8" comp both ends, MIP + FHT elbows | — |
| Everbilt 9 ft kit | 9 ft, garden-hose elbow | ~$32 |
| Fluidmaster B6W48 | 4 ft, 3/8" comp x 3/8" comp, elbow | — |

- Buy two. The spare costs less than a second trip from the Poconos.
- Avoid anything labeled 3/4" hose thread unless the valve is confirmed.
- Prefer 6 ft over 4 ft — slack is free, coming up short is not.

## 2. Dishwasher — 24" stainless tub

| Model | Price | dBA | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Midea 47 dBA (recommended) | ~$450-500 | 47 | cheapest true stainless tub |
| Frigidaire FDPC4314AS | ~$530-600 | 49 | stainless tub confirmed |
| Whirlpool Eco Series | ~$599-649 | 47 | stainless tub, best longevity |
| GE front control | ~$599 | 45 | quietest; tub material unconfirmed |
| Samsung smart | ~$579 | — | tub material unconfirmed |

**Recommendation: Midea at ~$475.** Real stainless tub, 47 dBA, cheapest in
category. For a rental the tub material and decibel rating are what matter;
smart features are wasted on guests. Whirlpool is the upgrade pick if the
extra ~$125 for longevity is worth it.

Do not buy the ~$299 Frigidaire: plastic tub, 62 dBA. "Stainless" there
refers to the door only. Plastic tubs stain and hold odor, and guests notice.

### Verify before buying

- [ ] Measure the cutout width (24" standard, but some kitchens are 18")
- [ ] Confirm tub material on the GE and Samsung — listings do not state it
- [ ] Confirm whether a power cord is included (most ship without one)
- [ ] Haul-away is $50 with delivery; free on some MyLowe's Pro Rewards tiers

**Septic note:** Milton is on septic. If the complaint is slow draining
rather than the machine itself, check the drain line first — a new
dishwasher will not fix a line problem.

**Repair vs replace:** past a spray arm or door gasket on an older unit, the
labor usually is not worth it in a rental at these appliance prices.
